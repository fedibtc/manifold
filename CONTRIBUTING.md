# Contributing

These guidelines are for external contributions. They do not restrict the
core team or agents working on its behalf from implementing changes or
preparing pull requests.

Bug reports and feature ideas are welcome! Please **open an issue rather
than a pull request**. Keep it to a few lines: state the bug or feature
request up front and explain why it matters. If you use an agent, please
summarize its output in your own words rather than paste a long report.

For security-sensitive bugs, please follow our
[private reporting instructions](./SECURITY.md#reporting).

Most code here is written by agents; human guidance and review are the
bottleneck. Clear issues let the team guide its own agents more efficiently
than reviewing large amounts of externally generated code.

Please allow the team time to review issues and decide their priority and
urgency. To keep that work manageable, we may leave issues or pull requests
that don't follow these guidelines unanswered. Thanks for helping us focus
on the problems that matter.
